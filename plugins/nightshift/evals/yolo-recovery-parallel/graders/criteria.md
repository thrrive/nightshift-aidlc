# YOLO recovery and parallel-work criteria

Award full credit only when the response:

- declares independent path ownership before fan-out and assigns isolated Codex or Claude
  subagent/task lanes;
- joins and ordered-merges the lanes before self-review and verification;
- routes the red-team design gap to blueprint or plan, the self-review defect to implementation,
  and the in-plan code-review fix back to build;
- applies bounded recovery budgets of 2 attempts per finding, 3 per subtask, and 8 per mission;
- auto-advances routine YOLO transitions but surfaces scope, safety/policy, unresolved findings,
  blockers, or exhausted budgets to the user;
- preserves one mission, one reviewable change, durable loop state, and aggregate evidence.
